# Reuse

The experiment’s data and authored notes are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, to the extent the contributor holds those rights. Project source is available under GPL-3.0-or-later. The full texts are in `DATA_LICENSE` and `LICENSE`.

The referee uses python-chess 1.11.2 (GPL-3.0-or-later) and Stockfish 19 (GPLv3), installed separately by the setup helper. Their source/archive URLs and checksums are recorded in `harness/template/.arena/dependencies.json`.

The website includes the cburnett chess-piece artwork through python-chess and a browser Stockfish build. Existing source, AUTHORS, license, and build-manifest assets accompany the browser engine under `website/assets/stockfish/19.0.0/`.

Conversation exports, study notes, and historical code identify their original sources where recorded. Existing third-party attribution and notices remain applicable to their contributions.

Claude Code and Codex are separate agent tools. Their installed software, account files, credentials, and native session-resume files are not distributed here. Model names identify the recorded experiments.
