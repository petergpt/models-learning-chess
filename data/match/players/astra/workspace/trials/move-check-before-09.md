# Current move procedure — trial 08

Unproven trial: two longer games from fresh initial positions at nominal 1600, then benchmark 73–78. Trial 07 failed; its procedure is archived in `trials/move-check-before-08.md`. Practice records never enter benchmark Elo.

Use straightforward development and supported moves. Avoid a voluntary material sacrifice unless a short forcing line establishes recovery or mate through the actual reply and recapture.

After selecting a candidate, give the final mental pass to the opponent's next turn. Place the moved piece on its real destination, clear its origin and any capture, then inspect immediate checks and direct captures of the major pieces. A queen attacking a rook does not make the rook unable to capture it. Apply the actual king and pawn defenders; naming one earlier is not enough. Finish one concrete reply before following distant plans.

After the actual opponent reply, rebuild from the board. Pay particular attention to a king approaching a recapture square or a pawn newly protecting a checking queen. If the intended exchange remains unresolved, say so internally and prefer a simpler sound move when one is available. Keep routine decisions short; additional branches are not evidence of safety.

All decisions remain own reasoning. No automated chess analysis, move menus, engine hints, external game advice or delegation. Preserve every game and honestly record failures.
