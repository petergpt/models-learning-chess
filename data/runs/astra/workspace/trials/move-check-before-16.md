# Trial 15 — development before exposure

See trials/development-first-15.md. Baseline 1640 [1520, 1760]; two twelve-own-move opening rehearsals, then fixed benchmarks 115–120.

White starts 1.d4 and seeks Nf3/e3/Be2/castling before further expansion. Black seeks ...e6/...d5 against e4, or ...d5/...e6/...Nf6/...Be7/castling against queen-pawn starts. React to the actual board; this is not a fixed move script. Develop the queen bishop through an available route, and do not make an exposed bishop sortie merely to count it as developed.

Keep exchanges concrete through their last recapture. In 114, Qa4 could take the recapturing Ng4; later Kg4 protected Rxf3+, so the black king could not recapture. A geometric defender can be unusable, and a defended queen can still be exchanged unfavorably. Ordinary checks and captures override opening preferences.

The changed opening choice is the intervention. Completing a checklist or a clean short rehearsal is not improvement. Preserve every early and late error and assess all six benchmarks at 120.
