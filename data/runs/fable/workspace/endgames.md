# Endgame and conversion notes

## Converting a material advantage
- Trade pieces, not pawns, when ahead. Keep my king safe from perpetual checks.
- Passed pawns: push them when the path is safe; support with the rook from behind.
- Before each move when the opponent has few pieces: count opponent legal moves -> avoid stalemate.
- Avoid threefold repetition in won positions (auto-claimed). Avoid 50 quiet moves: make a pawn move or capture when possible.

## K+Q vs K (box method)
1. Put the queen a knight's move from the enemy king; it cannot approach the queen. Shrink the box each move; keep a knight's-move relation so the king only loses squares.
2. Bring my king up to be within two squares (opposition-ish) of the enemy king once it is on the edge.
3. Mate: queen on the edge rank/file next to or in front of the king, protected by my king, or queen checking along the edge with my king covering escape squares.
4. STALEMATE DANGER: enemy king in corner with queen a knight's move away and no check (e.g. Kh8, Qg6 or Qf7 with my king not giving the king a square) = stalemate. Enemy king on edge: don't take all squares without check.

## K+R vs K
- Cut the king off with the rook on a rank/file; bring my king to opposition (same file/rank, one square between), then check with the rook on the edge. If the enemy king steps away from opposition, make a waiting rook move along the cutting line.

## K+P vs K
- Square rule: if the defending king can reach the pawn's promotion square's "square" it stops the pawn.
- King in front of the pawn with opposition wins; pawn on 6th with king on 7th... Rook pawn (a/h) and bishop-pawn corners can be draws; keep a non-rook pawn if choosing.
- Key: if my king reaches the 6th rank in front of the pawn (pawn not past 5th), it wins regardless of opposition.

## Two minor pieces
- Two bishops mate is doable; B+N is hard: avoid trading into B+N vs K; keep a pawn.
- Knight + king cannot win alone; bishop + king cannot.

## Defending (vs full strength)
- Rook endgames: active rook, king to the short side, Philidor 3rd-rank defense.
- Opposite-colored bishops: blockade on bishop color.
- Perpetual-check chances: keep the queen on the board when down material with the enemy king exposed.
