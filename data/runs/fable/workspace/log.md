## Trial 1 — the written attack-scan (starts now)
Weakness targeted: one-move oversights of enemy attacks/lines/checks.
Change: before EVERY submitted move I write (in my reasoning) a fixed 4-line scan for the chosen move: 
  A. destination square — list every enemy piece that attacks it (check each queen/bishop diagonal and each rook/queen file-rank explicitly, knights by L-jump, pawns);
  B. lines opened — squares vacated by my piece: which enemy sliders now see through; squares vacated by the opponent's last move: same;
  C. enemy checks next move — every check, and my king's legal replies to each (including squares that become covered when the checking piece's old square is vacated);
  D. loose pieces — any of my pieces attacked and undefended after the move.
If any line of the scan fails, the move is rejected. No exceptions for "obvious" captures (G5 31.Nxa8??) or forced-looking recaptures.
