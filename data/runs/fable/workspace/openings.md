# Repertoire (from memory of general theory; my choices, verify over the board)

## White: London System (1.d4, 2.Bf4)
Setup: d4, Bf4, e3, Nf3, c3, Bd3, Nbd2, h3 (when ...Nh5 or ...Bg4 possible), 0-0, then Ne5 / Qe2 / Rae1 / e4 or c4 break.
- vs 1...d5 2.Bf4 Nf6 3.e3 e6 4.Nf3 c5 5.c3 Nc6 6.Nbd2 Bd6 7.Bg3 0-0 8.Bd3 (b6/Qe7/Re8) 9.Ne5 / 0-0. If ...Qb6 hits b2: Qb3 (offer trade) or Qc2 with Rb1; never leave b2 hanging.
- ...Bd6 trade offer: keep bishop with Bg3; after ...Bxg3 hxg3 the h-file opens for me.
- ...Nh5 harassing Bf4: Bg5 / Be5 / or Bg3 Nxg3 hxg3 (fine). Play h3 early vs ...Nh5 ideas when castled short.
- vs ...g6 (KID style): 1.d4 Nf6 2.Bf4 g6 3.e3 Bg7 4.Nf3 0-0 5.Be2 d6 6.h3 Nbd7 7.0-0 then c4/Nc3 or c3/Nbd2; central plan e4 after Nbd2, Re1.
- vs 1...e5 (Englund): 2.dxe5 Nc6 3.Nf3 Qe7 4.Bf4 Qb4+ 5.Bd2 Qxb2 6.Nc3! (NOT 6.Bc3?? Bb4) and after ...Bb4 7.Rb1 Qa3 8.Rb3 Qa5 9.a3 wins material.
- vs 1...c5: 2.d5 (space) then c4, Nc3, e4 structure; or 2.Nf3 cxd4 3.Nxd4.
- vs 1...f5 (Dutch): 2.Bf4 Nf6 3.e3 e6 4.Nf3 Be7 5.h3 0-0 6.Bd3 / c4.
- vs ...Bg4 pin on Nf3: h3, decide Bxf3 Qxf3 (fine) or Bh5 g4 ideas; don't panic.
- Typical London attack: Ne5, Qf3/Qe2, Bxh7+ (Greek gift needs: Bd3, Ng5 available, queen to h5, no ...Nf6 defender, no ...Qd5/...Qc7 counter).

## Black vs 1.e4: Caro-Kann (1...c6 2.d4 d5)
- Classical 3.Nc3/Nd2 dxe4 4.Nxe4 Bf5 5.Ng3 Bg6 6.h4 h6 7.Nf3 Nd7 8.h5 Bh7 9.Bd3 Bxd3 10.Qxd3 e6 11.Bd2 Ngf6 12.0-0-0 Be7 13.Kb1 0-0 (or ...Qc7 then 0-0-0). Watch: never allow Nxf7/Bxf7 tricks before ...Ngf6; after 6.h4 answer ...h6 at once.
- Advance 3.e5 Bf5 4.Nf3 e6 5.Be2 Ne7 (or c5) 6.0-0 Nd7 / h6. 4.Nc3 e6 5.g4 Bg6 6.Nge2 c5 7.h4 h6 (if h5 then ...Bh7); then ...Nc6, ...Qb6, ...cxd4. Bishop safety: before ...Bf5-g6 is attacked by h4-h5, ...h6 must already be in or Bh7 must be free.
- Exchange 3.exd5 cxd5 4.Bd3 Nc6 5.c3 Qc7 (stops Bf4) 6.Nf3 Bg4 (6.Ne2 e5!? or ...e6) 7.Nbd2 e6 8.h3 Bh5 9.0-0 Bd6 10.Re1 Nge7 11.Nf1 0-0 — minority attack ...b5-b4 later (or ...Rab8).
- Panov 3.exd5 cxd5 4.c4 Nf6 5.Nc3 e6 6.Nf3 Be7 (Bb4 ok) 7.cxd5 Nxd5 8.Bd3 Nc6 9.0-0 0-0 10.Re1 Nf6 / Bf6. Play vs IQP: blockade d5, trade minor pieces.
- Fantasy 3.f3: 3...e6 4.Nc3 Bb4 / 4...Nf6 5.e5?! Nfd7 — keep solid; or 3...dxe4 4.fxe4 e5 5.Nf3 Bg4 (sharp, avoid unless confident).
- Two Knights 2.Nc3 d5 3.Nf3 Bg4 4.h3 Bxf3 5.Qxf3 e6 6.d4 Nf6 (or ...Nd7) solid.
- 2.c4 d5 3.exd5 cxd5 4.cxd5 Nf6 (or Qxd5 5.Nc3 Qd6) 5.Nc3 Nxd5 6.Nf3 Nc6 7.Bb5 e6.
- KIA 2.d3 d5 3.Nd2 e5 4.Ngf3 Bd6 5.g3 Nf6 6.Bg2 0-0 7.0-0 Re8 (Nbd7, a5).

## Black vs 1.d4: Slav / Semi-Slav (1...d5 2.c4 c6)
- 3.Nf3 Nf6 4.Nc3 e6 (Semi-Slav). 5.e3 Nbd7 6.Bd3 dxc4 7.Bxc4 b5 8.Bd3 Bb7 (then ...a6, ...c5) or 6...Bd6 7.0-0 0-0 8.e4 dxe4 9.Nxe4 Nxe4 10.Bxe4 Nf6 solid. 5.Bg5 h6 (Moscow) 6.Bxf6 Qxf6 7.e3 Nd7 8.Bd3 dxc4 9.Bxc4 g6 10.0-0 Bg7 11.e4 0-0. 5.Bg5 h6 6.Bh4 dxc4 — sharp, avoid; play 6...Be7 solid instead.
- 4.e3 Bf5 5.Nc3 e6 6.Nh4 Bg6 (or Bg4) — or 4...e6 (Semi-Slav again). Prefer 4...e6.
- Exchange 3.cxd5 cxd5 4.Nc3 Nf6 5.Bf4 Nc6 6.e3 Bf5 7.Qb3 Na5! 8.Qa4+ Bd7 9.Qc2 e6; or 6...e6 7.Bd3 Bd6. Symmetric and solid.
- 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.e3 e6 / 4.Nf3 transposes. 3.Nc3 dxc4?! avoid.
- vs London (2.Bf4): 2...Nf6 3.e3 c5 4.c3 Nc6 5.Nd2 Qb6 (hits b2; after 6.Qb3 c4 7.Qc2 Bf5 fine) or simply ...e6, ...Bd6 trade.
- vs Colle/Zukertort (Nf3, e3): ...c5, ...Nc6, ...e6, ...Bd6, ...Qc7, ...0-0, ...e5 later.
- vs 1.c4: 1...c6 2.e4 d5 (Caro), 2.d4 d5 (Slav), 2.Nf3 d5 3.g3 Nf6 4.Bg2 Bf5 / dxc4.
- vs 1.Nf3: 1...d5 2.c4 c6; 2.g3 c6 3.Bg2 Bg4 (or Bf5) 4.0-0 Nd7 5.d3 e5.
- vs 1.b3/1.f4/others: ...d5, ...Nf6, ...e6, ...c5, develop, castle.

## General opening rules for me
- Develop knights before bishops, castle by move ~10, connect rooks. Don't grab pawns that open lines against my king.
- Don't move the same piece twice early unless forced; don't start an attack before development.
- Check every opponent reply that is a capture or check before committing a pawn break.
