## Classification
Decisive error = one-move oversight of a mating check (back rank), the exact class Trial 1 targets, with the scan written but computed wrongly. The ending technique up to move 28 was good.

## Rules added (procedure v2.5)
- King flight squares: a king can never escape a rook/queen check by moving along the line of the check (same rank/file/diagonal). Flight squares along that line are NOT flight squares.
- Luft rule: when my king has three unmoved pawns in front of it and no piece guards my back rank, any enemy rook/queen that can reach my back rank with check is a mate threat; before any other plan, make luft or guard the rank.
