## Rule notes
- Referee repetition rule: the draw is claimed automatically as soon as the side to move has ANY legal move that recreates a position already seen twice. Therefore in a winning position never let any position (same side to move) occur twice: keep a written list of position signatures (piece squares + side to move) from the moment the ending starts, and before each move check that the resulting position is new AND that the opponent's possible replies do not recreate a twice-seen position.
- King-move legality: before any king move write every enemy rook/queen line through the destination square (rank and file, including adjacent squares).
