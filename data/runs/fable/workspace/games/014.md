# Game 14 — Black vs full-strength — LOSS (resigned at move 13, 0 illegal attempts)

1. e4 c6 2. d4 d5 3. e5 Bf5 4. h4 h6 5. g4 Bd7 6. Be3 e6 7. Nc3 c5 8. dxc5 Nc6 9. Nf3 Qa5 10. a3 Bxc5 11. b4 Nxb4 12. axb4 Bxb4?? 13. Rxa5 — resigned (queen lost for nothing).

## Course
- Caro-Kann Advance, 4.h4 line; 8.dxc5 won a pawn that 9...Qa5 (pinning Nc3) and 10...Bxc5 regained.
- 11.b4 hit queen and bishop. 11...Nxb4 was a reasonable try (12.Bxc5 Qxc5 13.axb4 Qxb4 is a knight for two pawns with a pin and an uncastled White king). White played 12.axb4 instead.
- 12...Bxb4?? ignored that the a-pawn's departure had opened the a-file: Ra1 attacked my queen on a5. The only square I checked for attackers of a5 were the nearby pieces (Be3, Nc3) and I wrote "a3 vacated: nothing". Correct was 12...Qxb4 (13.Bxc5 Qxc5, down a knight for two pawns, keeping the pin).

## Classification
One-move oversight of a long-range attacker along a line opened by the opponent's previous move — the exact class of games 3, 6, 7 — with the scan written but line B answered superficially. Cost: the queen.

## Rule added (procedure v2.8)
- Line A/D attacker enumeration is done BY LINE, not by nearby pieces: for each of my queen/rook/minor squares, name the piece (if any) at the far end of its file, its rank and both diagonals, plus knight hops. A capture or pawn advance by the opponent re-opens the file and both diagonals that pawn stood on — list those lines explicitly in line B ("the pawn that just moved from X unblocked: ...").
- Before any move that is not a response to a direct threat, first ask: which of my pieces is currently attacked? (Here the queen already was, and the move I chose did not address it.)
