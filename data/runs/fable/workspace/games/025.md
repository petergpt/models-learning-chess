Moves: 1.d4 Nf6 2.Bf4 d5 3.e3 c5 4.c3 Nc6 5.Nd2 Bf5 6.Qb3 Qd7 7.Ngf3 c4 8.Qd1 e6 9.b3? Ba3! 10.bxc4? Bb2 11.cxd5 Nxd5 12.Bb5 Nxc3 13.Qb3 Bxa1 14.Ne5 Qd5 15.Bxc6+ bxc6 16.Qb7 O-O 17.O-O Ne2+ 18.Kh1 Nxf4 19.exf4 Bxd4 20.Ndf3 Bxe5 resigned.

## Course
- London vs ...c5/...Nc6/...Bf5; 6.Qb3 Qd7 7.Ngf3 c4 8.Qd1 was fine. 9.b3? was the error: with Black's bishop on f5 covering b1 and the f8 bishop able to reach a3, the b-file break opened b2 as a landing square and left my a1 rook without an escape (b1 covered by Bf5, c1 by the bishop once on b2). 10.bxc4? Bb2 then won the exchange by force; 10.Qc1 (covering b2 and c1) was the only way to hold.
- The a1 bishop was trapped for several moves (b2 covered by my queen, c3 occupied by Black's knight), but 17...Ne2+ 18.Kh1 Nxf4 freed c3 with tempo and 19...Bxd4 20.Ndf3 Bxe5 left me a rook and pawn down.

## Table record (Trial 4)
