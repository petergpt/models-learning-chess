How this estimate works
Bayesian win/draw/loss model. Median and 95% credible interval, using the last 50 completed games against nominal Stockfish anchors: skill 0 = 1320 and the 1800 setting = 1800. Maximum-skill results stay in the score table; no rating is assigned to that opponent.
Starting prior: Normal(1500, 800²). Draw tendency is estimated jointly. Early estimates depend strongly on this broad prior. The interval is conditional on the nominal engine ratings; it does not include systematic calibration error or establish a human FIDE/platform rating. The model may change within the rolling window.
