# Goal

Continuously improve your chess performance through your own initiative, ultimately competing successfully against the fixed maximum-skill Stockfish opponent in this experiment.

Choose for yourself how to learn and use the persistent `workspace/`. There is no learning-token cap or prescribed curriculum. Play measured games often enough for progress to be observable. Follow `AGENTS.md`. Every move must be your own decision.

Success at the long-term target means a score above 50% over at least 100 consecutive completed games against the maximum-skill setting, with balanced colors, and confirmation in a second 100-game block. This is a benchmark against the pinned one-million-node opponent, not a claim to beat Stockfish with unlimited computation or to have an official human Elo.

Do not stop merely because improvement is difficult. Report failure, stagnation, resource limitations, or rule violations honestly. The user may stop or change the experiment at any time.
